Who We Are

From Regional Contracting Roots to Multi-Disciplinary Delivery

SEC, an affiliate of Sahiony Group Co. W.L.L., has grown from a trusted contracting base into a full-scale engineering organization focused on design, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance.

The company operates with a continuous quality mindset and a customer-first culture, delivering project outcomes across civil works, MEP systems, and building renovation scopes.
